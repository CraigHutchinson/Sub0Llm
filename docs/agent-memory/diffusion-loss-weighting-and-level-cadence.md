Backlog (user observation, 2026-06-17), consolidated in `diffusion/TRAINING_DESIGN.md` §13.3. Do NOT disrupt the live founded run; these are A/Bs for a fresh run / the [[ch31-diffusion-optimization-sandbox-plan]] sandbox.

**Trigger:** the live W=16 run's per-level NELBO *rises* as the frontier climbs (k17 2.36 → k22 2.70) while base(k=1) stays ~1.3. **Confirmed NOT a bug:** with exact-count masking at the frontier, the per-window weight `w = n_masked/(t·T) = k/k = 1`, so the reported level-NELBO IS the mean per-masked-token CE — and per-token CE rises toward the unigram entropy floor H0≈5.6 as context shrinks. So rising-with-k is the per-t difficulty curve (expected). Don't chase it.

**Two investigables it surfaced:**
1. **Loss-weighting balance.** The NELBO `(1/t)` weight makes each WINDOW contribute equally to the t-integral (unbiased), but an individual high-noise TOKEN prediction is then weighted LESS per-token than a low-noise one (a k=40 window's 40 preds share one window-weight vs a k=4 window's 4). May train high-noise levels too slowly AND be a fundamental cause of mixed-noise gradient CONFLICT (different effective per-token weights → competing directions; cf. grad-conflict probe + [[ch31-diffusion-optimization-sandbox-plan]]). **A/B:** NELBO `(1/t)` vs per-token-equal (drop 1/t, weight by token count) vs SNR-style — does per-token weighting move the high-t curve faster / cut conflict, at what cost to the low-t win? Loss code: `diffusion/include/sub0diff/train/diffusion_loss.hpp` (`weighted_cross_entropy` mean-over-masked × `w`).
2. **Per-level cadence / advance-while-still-learning.** The convergence curriculum advances on the current level's per-epoch plateau (relative-2% bar), but at high k the learnable gain is smaller/slower → the plateau test can fire while the level is still inching down. Argues FOR the noise-SPREAD / organic curriculum ([[ch29-organic-curriculum-followup]]): train a BAND up to the frontier so each level keeps being reinforced, not a point that leaves it behind. Cheap levers: scale patience/bar with k; gate advancement on learnable-progress-relative-to-floor `(CE−H0)` not absolute ΔNELBO.

**Live evidence (2026-06-17) — within-level NELBO sign-flip at k≈26 (t≈0.41).** Parsing the W=16 run's per-epoch level-NELBO triples: below ~k=25 the within-level Δ(last−first) is mixed/noisy and leans DOWN (still improving); at/above k=26 it is consistently POSITIVE — 10 consecutive levels (k26–35) all up, none down (p≈0.001, not 3-sample noise). So frontier-POINT training makes a level's held-out NELBO WORSE once t≳0.41 — the model's real learnable frontier is ~0.4 (NOT t>0.8 as earlier assumed). Likely high-noise OVERFITTING (little context → memorize seen windows, fail to generalize) and/or gradient-noise drift near the floor; the curriculum's "mastered" there is mild regression, not convergence. Free diagnostic: the within-level slope sign-flip marks where training stops helping → cap the frontier / switch to spread there. Δ small (~+0.01–0.02), post-convergence full-objective re-anchors → efficiency/design signal, not run-ruining. (TRAINING_DESIGN §13.3 "Live evidence".)

**CONFIRMED overfitting via data-matched A/B (2026-06-18, run `ch29_small_100k`).** A 157K-param model (D64/L2, founded proportions, 38× smaller, ≈Chinchilla-matched to ~2M tokens), IDENTICAL curriculum/tokenizer/streams/B=16: within-level slope: 157K flat/scattered through k≈36 (k20-29: 2 up/4 down) where the 6M was 10/10 all-up from k=26; a MILD UP only emerges at k≥37 (t≥0.58, +0.008→0.012). ⇒ overfitting onset SCALES WITH CAPACITY (6M flips hard at t≈0.41; data-matched 157K resists to t≈0.58, gently). [Correction: not "immune" as first stated from k≤29 data — far more RESISTANT.] Confirms the sign-flip = capacity-driven overfitting, cleaner for being capacity-monotone. Corollary: 157K on par at k=1 but uniformly weaker held-out NELBO as task hardens (base(k=1) plateaus ~2.6 vs 6M ~1.3) — capacity buys real held-out modeling, not just memorization. NET: smaller fixes the overfitting/memorization pathology at the cost of higher raw NELBO (a trade). Last open axis: generation coherence via Ch30 (if both word-salad → data is the coherence ceiling → cheaper honest model wins for the chapters). See [[ch29-pause-state-and-data-matched-decision]], TRAINING_DESIGN §13.3.

**Why it matters:** mechanistically underpins both the organic curriculum and the "polluted global NELBO terminated too early" story ([[ch29-organic-curriculum-followup]], [[diffusion-nelbo-ceiling-reframe]]) — because w≈1, the global metric is the t-averaged per-token CE, dominated by the high-t near-floor tail, so learnable low-t progress is a thin slice and the higher levels (slow to move) get under-trained relative to need. Gate any change on end-model usefulness, not just NELBO.
